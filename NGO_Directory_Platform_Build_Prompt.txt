# PROJECT: NGO DIRECTORY, SOCIAL IMPACT NETWORKING, AND EMERGENCY MEDICAL ASSISTANCE PLATFORM

## 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

Build a complete, production-oriented, full-stack web application called:

**NGO Directory and Social Impact Networking Platform**

The application is designed for a district-level government initiative to connect NGOs, donors, volunteers, injured individuals requiring financial assistance, and government administrators through a single centralized platform.

The project must contain two major modules:

1. NGO Directory and Social Impact Networking.
2. Emergency Medical Assistance and Direct Donations.

Use my existing MERN stack project structure and integrate the new functionality without breaking existing features.

Before writing code, inspect the existing project, identify its current implementation, and reuse existing components, authentication, database connections, API services, and styling wherever possible.

Do not unnecessarily recreate working features.

MEDICAL INFORMATION:
- Medical condition or injury.
- Brief explanation of the incident or treatment requirement.
- Hospital name.
- Hospital location.
- Treating department or contact, where appropriate.
- Estimated treatment cost.
- Amount of financial assistance required.
- Amount already received from other sources.
- Amount requested from the public.
- Treatment urgency.
- Required assistance deadline.

SUPPORTING DOCUMENTS:
- Medical reports.
- Hospital treatment estimate.
- Relevant prescriptions.
- Other documents required for verification.

Do not make medical documents publicly accessible. Restrict access to authorized administrators and the patient or representative. Use private storage and secure access controls.

### B. Verification workflow

Use the following campaign statuses:

- Draft
- Pending Verification
- Under Review
- Approved
- Rejected
- Active
- Paused

- Completed
- Closed

Implement status transitions carefully.

The administrator must review the submitted information and supporting documents before approving a campaign.

Only approved campaigns may become publicly visible or accept donations.

Display a clear verification badge on approved profiles.

Record the reviewer, review timestamp, decision, and reason for rejection.

Do not present uploaded documents or user-provided statements as independently verified until the relevant checks are completed.

### E. Donation system

Integrate Razorpay to support online donations.

The donation form should include:
- Donation amount.
- Optional donor name.
- Optional message.
- Anonymous donation option.
- Payment initiation.
- Payment confirmation.
- Receipt generation.

Support donations to:
1. An NGO.
2. An approved emergency medical assistance campaign.

Implement the complete payment lifecycle:

1. The frontend requests creation of a donation order.
2. The backend validates the amount and donation target.
3. The backend creates the payment order using Razorpay credentials.
4. The frontend opens the payment checkout.
5. The backend verifies the payment signature.
6. A verified webhook updates the payment status.
7. The donation is marked successful only after trusted server-side verification.
8. The campaign's confirmed raised amount is calculated from successful donations.
9. The donor can view the transaction and download the available receipt.

Use server-side payment verification and idempotent webhook handling.

Never trust an amount, payment status, or campaign total supplied directly by the frontend.

Store payment IDs, order IDs, transaction status, timestamps, and reconciliation information.

Do not store card numbers, CVVs, or other sensitive payment credentials.

Support the following donation transaction statuses:
- Created
- Pending
- Successful
- Failed
- Refunded
- Partially Refunded

Ensure repeated webhooks cannot count the same donation twice.

### F. Donation transparency

For every campaign, calculate:

Confirmed Amount Raised = Sum of eligible, successfully verified donations, adjusted for refunds.

Remaining Amount = Maximum of zero and Funding Target minus Confirmed Amount Raised.

Funding Progress = Confirmed Amount Raised divided by Funding Target, multiplied by 100, capped at 100%.

Calculate these values on the backend.

Display:
- Target amount.
- Confirmed amount raised.
- Number of successful donations.
- Remaining amount.
- Recent donation activity, respecting donor privacy.

Do not display pending or failed payments as successful donations.

Allow campaign owners to request closure when the target is reached or treatment is completed.

Handle overfunding and refunds according to a documented campaign policy.

### G. Direct donation to a patient

Support a clearly defined direct-donation model.

Initially, use the payment gateway to collect a donation assigned to a particular approved patient campaign. Store the association between the payment, campaign, and intended beneficiary.

If direct bank or UPI transfers to a patient are supported, provide an optional, explicitly consented payout destination and a secure verification workflow.

Never imply that the platform transferred money to a patient unless an actual transfer was completed and verified.

For direct transfers, distinguish:
- Donation pledged.
- External transfer reported.
- Transfer independently verified.
- Transfer not verified.

For gateway-based fundraising, document who receives and controls the collected funds, how payouts work, and how refunds or unused funds are handled.

Do not promise tax benefits or issue tax-deductible receipts unless the relevant legal eligibility requirements have been established.

### H. Fraud prevention and reporting

Implement:
- Campaign reporting.
- Duplicate campaign detection where practical.
- Document verification.
- Rate limiting.
- File type and file size restrictions.
- Private medical document storage.
- Access logging for sensitive files.
- Admin review history.
- Secure authorization for campaign edits.
- Audit logs for approval decisions and financial actions.

Never allow a user to approve their own campaign or edit another user's campaign.
